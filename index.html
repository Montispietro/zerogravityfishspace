Core Innovation

The Artificial
Fish Lung System

Our patented system recreates the essential conditions for aquatic life — buoyancy, oxygenation, pressure regulation — without large water volumes. The result: viable biological payloads for orbit, and radically efficient transport for live fish logistics on Earth.

Water Reduction

Proprietary fluid dynamics reduce water dependency by up to 90%, directly cutting payload mass, transport costs, and CO₂ emissions.

🛰️

Microgravity Stabilisation

A controlled fluid-film "biological water-suit" eliminates sloshing in zero-G, maintaining organism safety and behavioural integrity throughout launch and orbit.
